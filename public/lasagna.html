Lasagna 101

A basic, no‑nonsense guide to amazing homemade lasagna.

Layers of comfort, baked to perfection

Overview

Lasagna is built in layers: pasta sheets, a savory sauce, creamy cheese, and a melty top. This simple version keeps ingredients minimal, with reliable results.

  • Prep time: ~30 min
  • Cook time: 45–55 min
  • Serves: 6–8

Ingredients

  • 12 dry lasagna sheets
  • 1 lb (450 g) ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 24 oz (680 g) marinara or tomato sauce
  • 2 cups (475 g) ricotta
  • 2 cups (200 g) shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup (50 g) grated Parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper
  • Optional: fresh basil, oregano, red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375°F / 190°C.
  2. Brown meat in olive oil; add garlic, cook 30 sec. Stir in sauce; simmer 5–10 min. Season to taste.
  3. Mix ricotta with half the Parmesan; season with salt and pepper.
  4. Boil pasta per package until very al dente (or use no‑boil if available).
  5. Assemble in a 9×13 in (23×33 cm) pan: a thin layer of sauce, pasta, ricotta, mozzarella, sauce. Repeat 3–4 layers.
  6. Finish with sauce, remaining mozzarella, and Parmesan.
  7. Cover with foil (tent it) and bake 25–30 min; uncover and bake 10–15 min until browned and bubbly. Rest 10 min before slicing.

Tips

  • Let it rest: resting helps clean slices.
  • Season every layer lightly for balanced flavor.
  • Make‑ahead: assemble, cover, and refrigerate up to 24h; add ~10 min to bake time.
  • Freeze slices wrapped well for easy meals.

Serving idea: add a simple green salad and crusty bread.
